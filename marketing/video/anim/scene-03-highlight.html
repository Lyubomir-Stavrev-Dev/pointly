Why the best teams
present differently
Sarah Chen · 6 min read

The difference between a forgettable demo and one that lands a deal is rarely the product itself — it's how the presenter directs attention in real time.

The most effective remote presenters treat their screen the way a stage director treats the spotlight: deliberate, purposeful, always keeping the audience's gaze on what matters most.

Studies of attention in video calls show that viewers lose focus within 90 seconds when there is no visual anchor. Annotation tools change that — they give the presenter a way to say "look here" without saying a word.
